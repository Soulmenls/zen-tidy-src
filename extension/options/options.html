Model

Sonnet 5.5 groups tabs more accurately than Haiku 5.5, at about $0.10 for 250 tabs. Haiku 5.5 costs about a fifteenth of that but leaves more tabs ungrouped. Opus 5.5 costs more and takes longer.

Grouping

From level 2 up, Haiku 5.5 checks each larger group and splits any that mixes subjects. Levels 4 and 5 also leave a tab ungrouped when nothing else in its group is about the same thing, so more tabs stay loose. The checks add about 3 seconds and under a cent for 250 tabs.
